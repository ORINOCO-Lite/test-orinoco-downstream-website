    \ to describe archives that provide researchers with tools to store, share, and\

    \ are of interest to the neuroscientific community. The Brain Imaging Data Structure\
    \ (BIDS) and Neurodata Without Borders (NWB) are utilized by these archives to make\
    \ data more accessible to researchers by implementing a common standard. As the\
    \ necessity for integrating large-scale analysis into data repository platforms\
    \ continues to grow within the neuroscientific community, this article will highlight\
    \ the various analytical and customizable tools developed within the chosen archives\
    \ that may advance the field of neuroinformatics."
